# Open Heaven — storylines

20 s worship motion graphic, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Scripture on screen is KJV (public domain), quoted verbatim.
The One on the throne is never given a face or figure: only light, colour and the things scripture places around Him.

## A. A Door Standing Open (recommended)

The form is an endless zoom: one continuous dive through heaven after heaven. The device is Revelation 4 followed in order, so the viewer travels exactly where John was taken.
A night sky splits along one vertical seam of light. The camera goes through the door and down through layered heavens, ending before the throne. The viewer feels drawn in and upward, then still.

- **First 2 s:** dusk sky over a dark horizon. A thin vertical line of gold light cuts the sky top to bottom, and "a door was opened in heaven" types beside it, word by word.
- **Beats:** the door opens → the firmament: a crystal vault the camera breaks through (Ezek 1:22) → the wheels within wheels turning past, rims full of eyes (Ezek 1:18) → the sea of glass like crystal opens beneath (Rev 4:6) → the emerald rainbow arcs around a throne of jasper-and-sardine light (Rev 4:3), with seven lamps of fire before it (Rev 4:5) → "Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty" lands on screen → hold.
- **Look:** gemstone light, not glow. Jasper (#C9D6C2), sardius red (#A8322A) and emerald (#1F8A5B) light refracting through faceted crystal over a deep lapis ground (#14234A). Gold (#D9B45A) only on the door seam and the final line. Type is Cormorant Garamond at 600 weight.
- **Sound:** a low sustained pad that grows. A single choir swell when the door opens. The music lifts into full chords at the throne. A held chord under "Holy, holy, holy".

## B. Wheels Within Wheels

The device is the object as the explanation: Ezekiel 1's chariot-throne is the one hero object. The form is a flight through that one object.
The camera is inside Ezekiel's vision: rings "so high that they were dreadful," full of eyes, turning on every axis. Each ring the camera passes through opens a new dimension, until it rises above the firmament to the sapphire throne.

- **First 2 s:** a whirlwind of amber cloud (Ezek 1:4). A bronze rim the size of the frame rolls out of it, close enough to see the eyes along it.
- **Beats:** whirlwind → the four living creatures as wing silhouettes, wings touching (Ezek 1:9) → the dive through three nested wheels → above the crystal firmament → a throne "as the appearance of a sapphire stone" ringed by a rainbow (Ezek 1:26–28) → "This was the appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD" → hold.
- **Look:** burnished bronze (#B07A3A) and amber fire (#E8A13A) against storm cloud (#2B2A33), turning to sapphire (#1E4FA3) and a full rainbow at the top. Real 3D rings in Three.js with metal at 0.5 roughness.
- **Sound:** deep rumbling percussion, "like the noise of great waters" (Ezek 1:24). It cuts to sudden silence above the firmament, then one sustained choir note at the throne.

## C. The City Coming Down (bold)

The device is type carrying the argument. The form is one frame that rebuilds itself.
The words of Revelation 21 break apart and become architecture. The twelve foundation stones stack in their named colours, the gates of pearl open, and the gold street, "as it were transparent glass", runs toward the viewer. The New Jerusalem descends with no sun, because the Lamb is its light.

- **First 2 s:** the word "BEHOLD" fills the frame in white. Its letters split into twelve coloured stone blocks.
- **Beats:** BEHOLD breaks into the 12 foundations, jasper to amethyst (Rev 21:19–20) → the walls rise and the gates of pearl swing open (21:21) → the street of gold, transparent as glass → the city descends through cloud (21:2) → "Behold, I make all things new" (21:5) → hold.
- **Look:** a pale dawn ground (#EDE6D6) with flat colour blocks in the twelve stone colours. Pearl white and translucent gold are the only gradients. The display face is set huge.
- **Sound:** an anthem build on percussion. Each stone landing is a soft struck note in rising order. A full choir arrives when the gates open.
