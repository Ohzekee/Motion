# SPEC — Open Heaven (storyline A: "A Door Standing Open")

GOAL:    the viewer is carried up through the opened heavens of Revelation 4 and left still before the throne, with "Holy, holy, holy" on screen.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps
TIME:    20 s x 30 = 600 frames. No music bed in this build (no source available): the shot timing is the clock.

LOOK:    medium = cut gemstone and crystal: every light is a faceted, hard-edged plane (jasper, sardius, emerald, beryl, crystal), never a soft blur.
         ground by world: dusk lapis #14234A to #0B1530 (sky) · crystal #DCEBF2 / #A9C7D6 / #F4F1E6 (firmament)
         · lapis #14234A with amber fire #E8A13A at 35% (wheels) · pale jasper light #FFF6DE → #C9D6C2 over sea #DDE7E6 → #9DB7C2.
         ink: #F4EBD0 on dark worlds, #14234A on light worlds.
         accent: gold #D9B45A, only on the door seam; deep gold #7A5A12 only on the word "HOLY".
         gems: jasper #C9D6C2, sardius #A8322A, emerald #1F8A5B (+ #0F5C3D, #3FB27F, #7FD1A3, #CDEFD8), beryl/bronze #B8A04A / #8C6A2E.
         type: EB Garamond (bundled) 500, 72 px lines (6.7% of height), tracking 0; final "HOLY, HOLY, HOLY" 112 px caps, tracking +0.08em;
         small lines 44 px. Max 6 words a line.
         depth: perspective with focal 1400 px; no grain, no vignette, no bloom.
OBJECTS: the door: a gold seam that opens into an arched doorway in the night sky (Rev 4:1).
         the firmament: a vault of cut crystal facets, a tunnel of rings the camera flies up through (Ezek 1:22).
         the wheels: two bronze-beryl rings, one set crosswise in the other, turning on two axes, rims full of eyes (Ezek 1:16-18).
         the sea of glass: a crystal floor to the horizon, ruled into glass tiles that reflect the light (Rev 4:6).
         the throne: a jasper dais and seat; upon it no figure, only concentric facets of jasper-white and sardius-red light (Rev 4:2-3),
         a full emerald rainbow round about it (4:3), seven lamps of fire before it (4:5), twenty-four seats round about it (4:4).
MOTION:  enter = expo.out; camera dives = power3.in (zoom through); camera arrival = power3.out; colour/light sweeps = power2.inOut.
         Rotation of the wheels and the slow drift of crystal rings are linear (constant speed is the point).
         Titles land and stay dead still; exits are blur-out 0.2 s. Word stagger 0.08 s.
SHOTS:   (one continuous take; each world is entered through an aperture in the last)
  f0-78     (0-2.6 s)  night sky over dark hills; a gold seam splits the sky top to bottom. Read: "Behold, a door / was opened in heaven" (0.2-3.3 s), per-word blur-in.
  f78-138   (2.6-4.6 s) the seam opens into an arched doorway full of crystal light, then the camera dives through it (power3.in, x14). Read: "Come up hither" (3.6-5.6 s).
  f138-240  (4.6-8.0 s) inside the firmament: crystal rings stream past toward the light; a light band sweeps across the facets.
  f207-240  (6.9-8.0 s) the vault's centre breaks open; facets fling outward past the lens, revealing the wheels.
  f240-327  (8.0-10.9 s) the wheels turn on two axes, eyes along both rims; the camera dives through the hub (power3.in).
  f306-327  (10.2-10.9 s) an iris opens from the hub onto the sea of glass.
  f327-495  (10.9-16.5 s) gliding low over the sea of glass toward the light on the horizon; the throne rises out of it and the camera settles (power3.out). Read: "a sea of glass like unto crystal" (11.2-14.3 s).
  f438-480  (14.6-16.0 s) the emerald rainbow draws round about the throne; the seven lamps kindle one by one (15.2-16.0 s).
  f450-600  (15.0-20.0 s) "HOLY, HOLY, HOLY" (15.0) / "Lord God Almighty" (15.6) / "which was, and is, and is to come" (17.0); camera still; one light sweep across the sea (16.5-18.5 s); hold.
BANNED:  black void with small floating things; soft glow, bloom, lens flares, glowing orbs as decoration; floating particles;
         purple/blue gradient blobs, glass cards; every element fading and sliding up; titles that drift after landing;
         corner labels, frame borders, HUD; the cream-paper-serif-italic look; a figure or face on the throne.
